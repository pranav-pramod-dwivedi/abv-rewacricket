# The Founding Derby Teams (2)

The two permanent franchise clubs contesting the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Trophy.

 The Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament is contested exclusively as a marquee bilateral championship between **Destroyers Cricket Club (DES)** and **Dread Eleven (DE)**. Across 34 official matches from 2021 to 2026, the overall series titles stand level at 3&ndash;3.

### Destroyers Cricket Club

 Rewa, Madhya Pradesh &bull; Established 2021

3 Titles (2024, 2025, 2026 Reigning Champions)

The reigning three-time champion powerhouse of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, captained by premier all-rounder Pranav Dwivedi. Renowned for disciplined tactical bowling, explosive middle-order aggression, and a dominant 19-15 series rivalry record.

**Franchise Captain:** Pranav Dwivedi

**Primary Home Ground:** Divisional Cricket Stadium, Neem Chauraha / APSU Stadium

 Rewa, Madhya Pradesh &bull; Established 2021

3 Titles (2021, 2022, 2023 Champions)

**Primary Home Ground:** Martand School Ground No. 3 / Neem Chauraha Stadium
