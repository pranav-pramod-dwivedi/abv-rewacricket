Championship Final

Destroyers Clinch 2026 ABV Memorial Trophy in 12-Run Final Thriller

The 2026 Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament concluded in sensational fashion on Sunday evening as Destroyers Cricket Club defended their title with a nerve-shredding 12-run victory over arch-rivals Dread Eleven. Batting first, Destroyers posted 164/7 powered by Pranav Dwivedi's scintillating 82 off 44 balls. Despite a spirited 54 from Dread Eleven skipper Akhil Mishra, Destroyers' pace-spin battery closed out the death overs to seal a 3-2 series victory.

Issued by: RDCA Media & Press Desk
Official Circular

Governing Council Announces Upgraded 2026-27 Tournament Regulations

In a comprehensive executive session held at the Divisional Cricket Stadium in Neem Chauraha, Rewa, the Tournament Governing Council ratified an upgraded regulatory charter. The new policies include biometric PMOA access, mandatory VJD software parity for weather interruptions, and stricter anti-corruption reporting thresholds for all franchise participants.

Issued by: RDCA Media & Press Desk
Historical Telemetry

Top Performers Honour Roll: Pranav Dwivedi and Akhil Mishra Head All-Time Charts

Statistical verification across all 34 tournament derby matches reveals historic milestones. Destroyers skipper Pranav Dwivedi leads both aggregate run-scoring and bowling tallies, while Dread Eleven's Akhil Mishra remains the only player to feature in all championship campaigns with over 1,300 runs.

Infrastructure

Divisional Cricket Stadium at Neem Chauraha Undergoes Pitch & Pavilion Overhaul

Ground developments at the Divisional Cricket Stadium near Neem Chauraha, Rewa, have achieved full completion. Supported by MPCA infrastructural grants, the stadium now features three newly relayed pitches imported from Mumbai clay, modernized player dugouts, and compliant high-definition floodlight arrays.

Issued by: RDCA Media & Press Desk
Integrity Desk

Anti-Corruption Unit Deploys Digital Integrity Hotline for 2026 Cycle

Reaffirming its commitment to ethical sporting standards, the Tournament ACU has activated a 24/7 emergency integrity hotline (+91 7662 250011). Under tournament rules, failure to report corrupt overtures within 24 hours triggers mandatory disciplinary hearings and automatic suspensions.

Issued by: RDCA Media & Press Desk

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
