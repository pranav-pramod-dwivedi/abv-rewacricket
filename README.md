# Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament (abv-rewacricket)

> Official website and regulatory repository for the **Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament** (ABV Memorial Trophy), the premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, sanctioned by the **Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)** and governed under the auspices of the **Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA)**.

---

## 🏆 Tournament Overview

- **Competition Name**: Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament
- **Short Name**: ABV Memorial Trophy
- **Governing Body**: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) & Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA)
- **Primary Venue**: Divisional Cricket Stadium, Neem Chauraha, Boda Bagh Road, Rewa, MP 486001 (Near Neem Chauraha Hanuman Mandir)
- **Secondary / Championship Venue**: APSU Stadium Complex, Sirmour Road, Rewa, MP 486003
- **Reigning Champions (2026)**: Destroyers Cricket Club (DES) — Captain: Pranav Dwivedi (3 consecutive titles: 2024, 2025, 2026)
- **Historical Champions (2021–2023)**: Dread Eleven (DE) — Captain: Akhil Mishra (3 consecutive titles: 2021, 2022, 2023)
