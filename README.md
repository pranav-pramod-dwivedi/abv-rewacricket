# Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament (ABV Memorial Trophy)

> Official portal: **https://abv-rewacricket.pages.dev/**  
> Sanctioned by the **Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)** and affiliated with the **Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA)**.

---

## Tournament Overview

- **Official Website**: https://abv-rewacricket.pages.dev/
- **Competition**: Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament
- **Short Name**: ABV Memorial Trophy
- **Governing Body**: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) & Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA)
- **Primary Venue**: Divisional Cricket Stadium, Neem Chauraha, Boda Bagh Road, Rewa, MP 486001
- **Secondary Venue**: APSU Stadium Complex, Sirmour Road, Rewa, MP 486003
- **Format & Clashes**: 34 Matches across 6 Seasons (2021–2026) in One-Day (50 Overs) and T20
- **Series Status**: Deadlocked at **3–3** (Dread Eleven won 2021, 2022, 2023; Destroyers CC won 2024, 2025, 2026)

---

## The 2 Marquee Franchises

The tournament is contested exclusively between the two premier invitational cricket clubs of Rewa Division:

1. **Destroyers Cricket Club (DES)**
   - Captain: Pranav Dwivedi
   - Championship Titles: 3 (2024, 2025, 2026)
   - Official Franchise Portal: https://destroyers-rewacricket.pages.dev/
2. **Dread Eleven (DE)**
   - Captain: Akhil Mishra
   - Championship Titles: 3 (2021, 2022, 2023)
   - Official Franchise Portal: https://dread-eleven-rewacricket.pages.dev/
