Destroyers Cricket Club

The reigning three-time champion powerhouse of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, captained by premier all-rounder Pranav Dwivedi. Renowned for disciplined tactical bowling, explosive middle-order aggression, and a dominant 19-15 series rivalry record.

Dread Eleven Cricket Club

Rewa, Madhya Pradesh • Est. 2021
3 Titles (2021, 2022, 2023 Champions)

The fierce, relentless arch-rivals of Destroyers CC, captained by top-order talisman Akhil Mishra. Inaugural winners of the tournament with three consecutive titles from 2021 to 2023, playing an aggressive, high-octane brand of cricket.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
