Participating Teams & Franchises

Official directory of all 12 premier clubs and invitational franchises competing in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament circuit, complete with official emblems, home grounds, titles, and verified external portals.

Chennai Super Kings

Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Home: M. A. Chidambaram Stadium (Chepauk)
5 Titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023)

The Chennai Super Kings are one of the most successful franchises in world cricket, renowned for disciplined execution, fierce loyalty, and five championship titles.

Visit Official Website →

Delhi Capitals

New Delhi, Delhi
Home: Arun Jaitley Stadium
Runners-up (2020)

Representing the national capital territory, Delhi Capitals boast a dynamic blend of Indian young talent and international power hitters.

Visit Official Website →

Gujarat Titans

Ahmedabad, Gujarat
Home: Narendra Modi Stadium
1 Title (2022 Champions, 2023 Finalists)

Based out of the world's largest cricket stadium in Ahmedabad, Gujarat Titans clinched championship glory in their inaugural season.

Visit Official Website →

Kolkata Knight Riders

Kolkata, West Bengal
Home: Eden Gardens
3 Titles (2012, 2014, 2024)

Hailing from the historic Eden Gardens, KKR are three-time champions celebrated for their aggressive cricket culture and global brand identity.

Visit Official Website →

Lucknow Super Giants

Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
Home: Bharat Ratna Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee Ekana Cricket Stadium
Playoffs (2022, 2023)

Playing at the grand Atal Bihari Vajpayee Ekana Stadium in Lucknow, LSG represent the heartland of North India with high-octane tactical brilliance.

Visit Official Website →

Mumbai Indians

Mumbai, Maharashtra
Home: Wankhede Stadium
5 Titles (2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020)

Five-time champions Mumbai Indians have established a legacy of scouting brilliance, tactical innovation, and unmatched silverware at Wankhede.

Visit Official Website →

Punjab Kings

Mohali / Mullanpur, Punjab
Home: Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium
Finalists (2014)

The proud voice of the north, Punjab Kings bring fearless spirit, aggressive batting arsenals, and deep community support across Punjab.

Visit Official Website →

Rajasthan Royals

Jaipur, Rajasthan
Home: Sawai Mansingh Stadium
1 Title (Inaugural 2008 Champions)

The inaugural champions of 2008, Rajasthan Royals are pioneers in moneyball analytics, fearless powerplay hitting, and royal heritage.

Visit Official Website →

Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Bengaluru, Karnataka
Home: M. Chinnaswamy Stadium
3-time Finalists (2009, 2011, 2016)

Backed by one of the most passionate fanbases in global sports, RCB command Chinnaswamy Stadium with electrifying entertainment and explosive batting.

Visit Official Website →

Sunrisers Hyderabad

Hyderabad, Telangana
Home: Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium
1 Title (2016 Champions, 2024 Finalists)

Sunrisers Hyderabad are revered for record-shattering power batting, lethal bowling attacks, and relentless aggressive brand of T20 play.

Visit Official Website →

Destroyers Cricket Club

Rewa, Madhya Pradesh
Home: Divisional Cricket Stadium, Neem Chauraha / APSU Stadium
3 Titles (2024, 2025, 2026 Reigning Champions)

The reigning three-time champion powerhouse of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, captained by premier all-rounder Pranav Dwivedi.

Visit Official Website →

Dread Eleven Cricket Club

Rewa, Madhya Pradesh
Home: Martand School Ground No. 3 / Neem Chauraha Stadium
3 Titles (2021, 2022, 2023 Champions)

The fierce arch-rivals of Destroyers CC, captained by top-order talisman Akhil Mishra with an unrelenting athletic brutalist identity.

Visit Official Website →

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
