# Anti-Doping Code

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 WADA and NADA compliant anti-doping code ensuring fair athletic competition through rigorous in-competition and out-of-competition testing.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

It is each athlete&#39;s personal duty to ensure that no prohibited substance enters his or her body. Athletes are responsible for any prohibited substance or its metabolites found in their samples.

### :

The tournament adopts the current World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) Prohibited List, covering anabolic agents, peptide hormones, beta-2 agonists, diuretics, and stimulants.

### :

Doping control officers may conduct random or targeted urine and blood sample collection at any match venue (APSU Stadium, Neem Chauraha Stadium, Martand Ground) immediately following match completion.

### :

Players requiring prescribed medication containing prohibited substances must submit a formal TUE application to the Tournament Medical Board at least 21 days before competing.

### :

First violation results in a mandatory 2 to 4-year suspension. Aggravating circumstances or intentional tampering result in lifetime disqualification.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
