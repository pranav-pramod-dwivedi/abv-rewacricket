# Media Accreditation Terms and Conditions

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Application protocol, eligibility criteria, bib collection, and venue privileges for print, electronic, and digital journalists.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

Journalists and photojournalists must submit official accreditation requests with editor endorsement at least 7 days before opening match day.

### :

Pitch-side photojournalists must wear the official high-visibility numbered bib at all times while in the photographers&#39; trench.

### :

Accredited media must observe professional decorum during post-match press conferences and follow the moderation of the Press Officer.

### :

The Tournament Governing Council reserves the right to immediately revoke accreditation for breaches of PMOA rules or unauthorized streaming.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
