# News Access Regulations

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Rules governing the access, capture, editing, and distribution of match footage, audio, and news by non-rights holder media networks.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

Non-rights holder broadcast networks may use up to 90 seconds of match footage per news bulletin, with a maximum of 6 minutes across a 24-hour broadcast cycle.

### :

No non-rights holder may broadcast or stream live or near-live moving video footage from the stadium during the continuance of a match.

### :

All match clips utilized in news broadcasts must carry an on-screen courtesy bug: &#39;Footage Courtesy: Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament / RDCA&#39;.

### :

Video clips may not be posted natively to third-party social media handles without formal digital sub-licensing authorization.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
