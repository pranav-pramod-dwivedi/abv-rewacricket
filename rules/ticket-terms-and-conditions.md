# Ticket Terms and Conditions

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Conditions of entry, prohibited articles, spectator code of conduct, rain-check refund policy, and stadium evacuation protocols.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

Possession of a valid ticket, pass, or digital QR code entitles the holder entry into the specified enclosure of the stadium. Admission is revocable at the discretion of venue management.

### :

Glass bottles, metal cans, firecrackers, laser pointers, weapons, alcoholic beverages, commercial recording cameras, and political banners are strictly barred at turnstiles.

### :

Spectators must occupy only the allocated stand or seat. Entering the field of play, throwing objects onto the turf, or using abusive language will lead to immediate arrest and prosecution.

### :

If play is abandoned without a single ball bowled on the match date, tickets are eligible for full refund within 7 working days. If 5 overs or more are completed, no refund is payable.

### :

Ticket holders consent to physical baggage inspection, body frisking by security personnel, and being recorded by stadium CCTV cameras.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
