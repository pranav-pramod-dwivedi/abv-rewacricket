REG-09 • Discipline & On-field Behavior

Code of Conduct for Players and Team Officials

Effective Seasons: 2021–2026 • Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

Comprehensive 4-tier disciplinary framework penalizing dissent, ball-tampering, abusive language, equipment damage, and physical assault.

Codified Articles & Clauses

:

Excessive appealing, aggressive hand gestures, kicking the boundary rope, dissent at umpire decisions. Sanction: Official reprimand up to 50% match fee fine.

:

Serious dissent, obscene language directed at an opponent, throwing the ball deliberately near a player, ball tampering. Sanction: 50% to 100% match fee fine and/or 1-match suspension.

:

Intimidation of match officials, threatening physical assault against opponents or spectators. Sanction: 2 to 4-match suspension.

:

Physical assault on the field, acts of violence, refusal to play, bringing the tournament into grave disrepute. Sanction: 1-year suspension up to permanent tournament expulsion.

:

Level 2, 3, and 4 charges are adjudicated by the Match Referee in an on-venue hearing conducted within 24 hours of match conclusion with right of legal representation.

Enforcement & Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
