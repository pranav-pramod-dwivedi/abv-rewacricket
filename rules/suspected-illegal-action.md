# Suspected Illegal Bowling Action Policy &amp; Guidelines

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Standardized procedure for reporting, scrutinizing, and biomechanically evaluating bowling actions exceeding the permissible 15-degree elbow extension limit.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

A bowling action is deemed legal if the bowler&#39;s elbow joint does not extend by more than 15 degrees from the moment the bowling arm reaches horizontal until the release of the ball.

### :

If both on-field umpires or the TV umpire suspect a bowler&#39;s action, they must submit an official Match Officials&#39; Report to the Match Referee within 2 hours of match conclusion.

### :

Upon the first report, the bowler and team management receive official footage. The player may continue bowling for 14 days pending laboratory assessment at an accredited biomechanics center.

### :

If scientific analysis confirms an elbow extension exceeding 15 degrees, or upon a second formal reporting during the tournament, the player is suspended from bowling in all tournament fixtures until cleared.

### :

Suspended bowlers may undergo remedial coaching with certified RDCA/MPCA bowling consultants before applying for independent re-testing.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
