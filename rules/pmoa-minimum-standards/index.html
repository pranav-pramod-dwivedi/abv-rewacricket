REG-07 • Integrity & Security

PMOA (Players & Match Officials Area) Minimum Standards

Effective Seasons: 2021–2026 • Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

Mandatory security cordon and communication blackout safeguarding the integrity of dressing rooms, dugouts, and match officials' facilities.

Codified Articles & Clauses

:

The PMOA encompasses player dressing rooms, dugouts, viewing terraces, match referee suites, umpire change rooms, and designated tunnel passageways.

:

All mobile phones, smart watches, laptops, two-way radios, and transmission equipment must be deposited with the Tournament Anti-Corruption Manager 60 minutes before the toss.

:

Only personnel holding valid PMOA accreditation badges (players, coach, physio, doctor, match officials) are permitted entry. Visitors must register in the official PMOA logbook.

:

Entry doors and corridors leading to PMOA suites are monitored by 24-hour recorded CCTV feeds under the custody of the Chief Security Officer.

Enforcement & Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
