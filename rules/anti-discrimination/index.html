Official Policy Overview

Affirmative commitment to equal dignity and fair treatment regardless of religion, caste, creed, region, disability, or socio-economic background.

1. Equal Opportunity

All players, officials, and academy candidates shall be evaluated solely on merit, skill, fitness, and conduct without bias based on religion, caste, linguistic background, or state of origin.

2. Harassment & Bullying

Verbal intimidation, derogatory nicknames, or exclusionary dressing-room behaviour aimed at a participant's socio-cultural identity will trigger immediate inquiry by the Disciplinary Committee.

3. Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Any registered individual may lodge a confidential discrimination complaint to the Tournament Ombudsman within 14 days of an incident. Inquiries must be completed within 10 days.
