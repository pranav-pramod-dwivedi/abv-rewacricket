REG-03 • Inclusion & Social Conduct

Anti-Racism Policy

Effective Seasons: 2021–2026 • Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

Zero-tolerance framework prohibiting racial slurs, derogatory chants, or discrimination on the basis of race, colour, or ethnic origin.

Codified Articles & Clauses

:

The Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament operates a strict zero-tolerance policy towards racist behaviour by players, coaching staff, venue personnel, or spectators.

:

Any player uttering racist slurs or making offensive gestures will be immediately charged under Level 3 of the Code of Conduct, incurring an immediate minimum 4-match suspension.

:

Spectators engaging in racist chants, insults, or harassment will be immediately ejected from the stadium by venue security and handed over to local police authorities under relevant Indian Penal Code provisions.

:

Umpires possess the authority to pause match proceedings if crowd abuse persists, and instruct the ground announcer to issue formal public warnings before restarting.

Enforcement & Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
