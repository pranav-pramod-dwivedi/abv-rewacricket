# Anti-Corruption Policy &amp; Protocols

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Mandatory anti-corruption code for players, match officials, and team personnel to safeguard against match-fixing, spot-fixing, and illegal betting.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

No participant shall fix, contrive, or improperly influence the result, progress, conduct or any aspect of any tournament match. Receiving or seeking any bribe, gift, or consideration in return for non-performance or disclosure of inside information is strictly prohibited.

### :

Every player, official, or team staff member approached by any person to engage in corrupt conduct must report the approach to the Anti-Corruption Unit (ACU) within 24 hours. Failure to report carries an automatic minimum 1-year ban.

### :

All participants are strictly barred from betting directly or indirectly on any match in the tournament, or providing pitch, selection, or tactical information to third parties prior to public toss announcement.

### :

The Tournament Anti-Corruption Officer is empowered to request itemized phone records, bank statements, electronic devices, and summon participants for recorded interviews.

### :

Sanctions range from a minimum 2-year ban for betting offenses up to a lifetime ban from all RDCA, MPCA, and BCCI-sanctioned cricket for proven match manipulation.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
