# Image Use Terms &amp; Photography Guidelines

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Legal terms licensing still photographic imagery for accredited journalists and strictly forbidding commercial merchandise exploitation.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

Photographs taken inside tournament stadiums by accredited photographers may be utilized strictly for editorial news reporting and sports journalism.

### :

Images cannot be sold, sublicensed, or reproduced on commercial posters, digital trading cards, NFT assets, apparel, or promotional endorsements without written consent.

### :

The collective image rights of participating athletes remain protected under the Tournament Participant Agreement and statutory Indian personality rights law.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
