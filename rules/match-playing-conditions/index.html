Policy Overview

Complete playing regulations governing match duration, over rates, field restrictions, powerplays, DRS reviews, ball specifications, and tie-breakers.

1. Ball Specifications

All white-ball matches are conducted using four-piece certified SG White Club/Tournament turf balls. In 50-over matches, two new balls are used per innings, one from each end.

2. Powerplay & Fielding Restrictions (T20)

Overs 1-6: Mandatory Powerplay (maximum 2 fielders outside 30-yard circle). Overs 7-20: Maximum 5 fielders outside the circle. Maximum 4 overs per bowler.

3. Powerplay Restrictions (50 Overs)

P1 (Overs 1-10): Mandatory (max 2 fielders outside). P2 (Overs 11-40): Max 4 fielders outside. P3 (Overs 41-50): Max 5 fielders outside. Maximum 10 overs per bowler.

4. Over Rates & Penalties

Teams must complete 20 overs within 85 minutes. If a team fails to start the final over by the scheduled cut-off time, one additional fielder must be brought inside the 30-yard circle for the remainder of the innings.

5. Super Over Protocol

In the event of a tied match, a Super Over will be played. If the Super Over is tied, subsequent Super Overs will be played until a clear winner emerges.

6. Rain Interruption & VJD Method

A minimum of 5 overs per side in T20 and 20 overs per side in 50 overs must be bowled to constitute a result. Revised targets are calculated using the certified VJD / DLS mathematical software.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
