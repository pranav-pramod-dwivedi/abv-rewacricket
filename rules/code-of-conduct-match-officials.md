# Code of Conduct for Match Officials

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Standards of professionalism, impartiality, physical fitness, conflict of interest disclosure, and report writing for umpires and match referees.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

Officials must execute their duties with complete neutrality and never express bias or favouritism towards any team or athlete.

### :

Officials must disclose any commercial, familial, or personal relationship with competing clubs or players prior to appointment.

### :

Umpires and referees are forbidden from discussing decisions, player behaviour, or pitch conditions with media representatives or on social media platforms.

### :

The Match Referee must submit the complete official scorecard, pitch rating, over-rate tally, and captain&#39;s assessment within 12 hours of match finish.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
