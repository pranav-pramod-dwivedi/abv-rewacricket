Policy Overview

Standardized procedure for reporting, scrutinizing, and biomechanically evaluating bowling actions exceeding the permissible 15-degree elbow extension limit.

1. Legal Bowling Action Definition

A bowling action is deemed legal if the bowler's elbow joint does not extend by more than 15 degrees from the moment the bowling arm reaches horizontal until the release of the ball.

2. Match Official Reporting

If both on-field umpires or the TV umpire suspect a bowler's action, they must submit an official Match Officials' Report to the Match Referee within 2 hours of match conclusion.

3. Warning & Video Review

Upon the first report, the bowler and team management receive official footage. The player may continue bowling for 14 days pending laboratory assessment at an accredited biomechanics center.

4. Suspension from Bowling

If scientific analysis confirms an elbow extension exceeding 15 degrees, or upon a second formal reporting during the tournament, the player is suspended from bowling in all tournament fixtures until cleared.

5. Remedial Coaching

Suspended bowlers may undergo remedial coaching with certified RDCA/MPCA bowling consultants before applying for independent re-testing.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

The premier invitational cricket championship of Vindhya Pradesh, honoring former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee. Contested through the iconic 34-match derby between Destroyers Cricket Club and Dread Eleven. Officially sanctioned by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) and affiliated with Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA).
