# Clothing and Equipment Regulations

Effective Seasons: 2021&ndash;2026 &bull; Authority: Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA)

Statutory Tournament Regulation

 Standardization of coloured clothing, uniform design, sponsor logo dimensions, protective gear safety standards, and bat gauge compliance.

## Codified Articles &amp; Clauses

### :

All players in a team must wear identical coloured jerseys and trousers. White jerseys are strictly forbidden in white-ball day-night or day encounters.

### :

Player name must appear in uppercase across the upper back (minimum 6cm height). Player jersey number must appear centered below (minimum 25cm height).

### :

Principal chest sponsor logo cannot exceed 250 sq cm. Sleeve sponsor logos cannot exceed 65 sq cm each. Non-commercial club emblems cannot exceed 50 sq cm.

### :

Helmets worn while batting against pace bowlers or wicket-keeping up to the stumps must strictly comply with the British Standard BS 7928:2013 specification.

### :

All bats must pass through the official bat gauge: maximum width 10.8 cm, maximum depth 6.7 cm, and maximum edge 4.0 cm.

### Enforcement &amp; Confidential Inquiries

Violations of this regulation are subject to expedited review by the RDCA Disciplinary Committee. For confidential reporting, contact the Integrity Officer.
